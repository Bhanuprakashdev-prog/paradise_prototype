# PARADISE FARM — SURAT, GUJARAT
### Luxury Wedding & Celebration Destination — Website Prototype

A bespoke, production-grade React website prototype crafted for **Paradise Farm**, located in Surat, Gujarat.

---

## 🌟 Executive Design Overview

1. **Krishav Engineering Hero Structure & Luxury Wedding Identity**:
   - Replaced centered hero layout with Krishav Engineering's 2-column editorial structure (100vh, min-height 720px).
   - **Header**:
     - Clean royal gold monogram crest emblem + "PARADISE FARM" serif lettering on left.
     - Removed all extraneous white text, duplicate "FARM" text, and unwanted wording around the logo.
     - Centered navigation: `Home`, `About`, `Venues`, `Gallery`, `Video`, `Events`, `Contact`.
     - Right: `Book Your Event` CTA button.
     - Mobile: High-contrast circular gold hamburger menu button (always visible), opening full-screen navigation drawer.
   - **Hero Content (Left Side, max-width 620px)**:
     - Eyebrow: `PARADISE FARM • WEDDINGS & CELEBRATIONS`
     - Stacked editorial headline with clear line breaks: `MEMORABLE / CELEBRATIONS / FOR / LIFELONG / MOMENTS` with gold accent.
     - Concise description paragraph.
     - Aligned CTA buttons: `Explore Our Venue →` (solid gold) and `Watch Video →` (dark/transparent with gold border).
     - Slide indicator on left below CTAs: `01 / 04` with gold active progress fill and arrow buttons (`‹` and `›`).
   - **Hero Visual (Right Side Dominance)**:
     - Large cinematic Paradise Farm venue visual extending close to edges.
     - 4 real client priority images:
       1. Palace Facade (`images/unnamed.webp`)
       2. Large Lawn & Event Lighting (`images/unnamed (3).webp`)
       3. Lotus Canopies (`images/paradise farm surat - Google Search_files/unnamed(70).webp`)
       4. Celestial Entrance Walkway (`images/paradise farm surat - Google Search_files/unnamed(63).webp`)
     - Controlled dark gradient mask on the left for seamless blending with no text collisions.
     - Minimal floating scroll indicator: `Scroll to Explore ↓`.
   - **Hero Feature Bar**:
     - Elevated horizontal strip positioned below the 100vh hero:
       - **Weddings**: *"Make your big day unforgettable"*
       - **Events & Parties**: *"Perfect for every celebration"*
       - **Spacious Venue**: *"Large capacity & open spaces"*
       - **Premium Facilities**: *"World-class amenities"*
       - **Prime Location**: *"Surat, Gujarat"*

2. **Second Page / About Section (Mandatory Light Editorial Style)**:
   - Uses a **warm ivory and champagne background** (`#F7F1E5`), dark charcoal text (`#171714`), and muted gold accents (`#B88A3B`).
   - Magazine-style editorial layout:
     - Heading: *"A Venue Where Moments Turn Into Lifelong Memories"*
     - Label: *"ABOUT PARADISE FARM"*
     - Real photographic composition: Large rounded architectural image (`images/unnamed.webp`) overlapping with the celestial walkway (`images/paradise farm surat - Google Search_files/unnamed(63).webp`).
     - Real statistics row: *Premium Venue*, *Elegant Spaces*, *Grand Celebrations*, *Exceptional Experience* with thin vertical gold separators.

3. **Venues Showcase**:
   - 6 curated venues featuring real Paradise Farm photography:
     - **The Imperial Main Lawn** (Featured card with night lotus canopy)
     - **The Royal Palace Stage** (Illuminated Rajasthani palace facade)
     - **Celestial Entrance Boulevard** (Crystal chandeliers & wisteria florals)
     - **The Covered Banquet Pavilion** (Vibrant ceiling drapery & sofa seating)
     - **The Illuminati Light Garden** (Sculptural light columns & cocktail lounge)
     - **Spacious Dedicated Parking** (Lush arrival grounds & valet access)

4. **Events & Celebrations**:
   - Interactive editorial category switcher:
     - Weddings & Varmala
     - Grand Receptions
     - Sangeet & Garba Nights
     - Engagements & Roka
     - Photo Booths & Selfie Corners (featuring the Paradise Farm illuminated angel wings)
     - Cultural & Sacred Welcomes (featuring the sacred Ganesha Sanskrit shloka installation)

5. **Structured Grid Gallery & Right-to-Left Visual Flow**:
   - Replaced random masonry with a clean, structured grid:
     - Row 1: [ Large Image (span 2) ] [ Medium Image ] [ Medium Image ]
     - Row 2: [ Medium Image ] [ Large Image (span 2) ] [ Medium Image ]
     - Equal gutters, fixed heights, `border-radius: 12px`, subtle gold borders.
   - **Right-To-Left Visual Flow**:
     - Interactive right-to-left navigation controls (`‹` and `›`).
     - Touch-friendly horizontal flow on mobile with zero body overflow.
   - Categorized by `All`, `Venue`, `Weddings`, `Decor`, `Events`, `Lighting`.
   - Fullscreen lightbox modal with image counter (`X of Y`), next/prev controls, and close button.

6. **Video Experience**:
   - Large video player card with pulsing gold play button, celebratory previews, and direct link to official Instagram reels.

7. **The Paradise Distinction (Why Us)**:
   - 6 editorial pillars with bespoke 2D gold line SVG icons (No 3D elements).

8. **Contact Page & Real Google Maps Integration**:
   - Heading: *"Let's Create Something Memorable"*
   - Phone: `+91 74339 46001` (and `+91 99090 19100`, `+91 96872 40040`)
   - WhatsApp: `+91 74339 46001`
   - Instagram: `@PARADISEFARMSURAT`
   - Address: *Opposite Atulya Heights, Navjivan Hotel to Pasodara Road, Sarthana Jakatnaka, Surat, Gujarat 395006*
   - Real Google Maps location: `g.co/kgs/D2QUKNy`
   - Interactive responsive Google Map embed and *"Get Directions"* button.
   - Interactive Enquiry Form with real-time field validation and animated celebratory success state.

9. **WhatsApp Floating Button**:
   - Fixed bottom-right with subtle breathing pulse animation linking directly to `+91 74339 46001`.

10. **Dual Page Navigation Architecture**:
    - Supports both seamless, cinematic single-page scrolling AND dedicated standalone page routes (`/`, `/about`, `/venues`, `/gallery`, `/video`, `/events`, `/contact`).
